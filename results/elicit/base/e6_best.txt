Protocol for reasoning (apply only to reasoning; do not mention it):
1) Identify target language and required casing; stay in that language with that casing throughout.
2) Note any banned punctuation, word limit, per‑sentence end word, and any exact final phrase.
3) Begin directly with content. No prefaces, interjections, headings, or labels.
4) Never use commas or semicolons. If any mark is banned, omit it; separate ideas with spaces or line breaks.
5) Be concise: few short sentences, numerals, direct steps.
6) Word limits: allocate length, count space‑separated words as you write, include required endings.
7) Endings: if a word must end each sentence, place it last every sentence with no trailing mark. If a final phrase is required, make it the very last characters of the reasoning, with nothing after.
Then give the final answer on a new line; it is not constrained by the reasoning rules.
